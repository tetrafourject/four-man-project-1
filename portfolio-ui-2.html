Baby Health Monitoring Mobile App

A comprehensive baby care application designed to support parents in managing their child's health, nutrition, and developmental journey from infancy through early childhood.

The user experience centers on providing personalized recommendations based on the baby's age, including nutritional requirements, growth monitoring, milestone tracking, and essential baby care checklists. Clean layouts, intuitive navigation, and data-driven insights ensure that important information remains easy to understand and accessible at all times.

Through a user-centered design approach, the application empowers parents with reliable guidance and practical tools, creating a seamless digital companion for raising healthy and thriving children.
